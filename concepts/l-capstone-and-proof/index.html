Capstone and proof

The capstone turns 48 labs into one story you can tell in ten minutes, backed by a public repository.

Key ideas

  1. One command builds, validates and tears down a cluster and writes a dated report against a criteria file.
  2. Injected failures prove the kit works: a simulated XID, EFA disabled, wrong NUMA binding, a power-capped straggler.
  3. The README sells it: architecture diagram, results, cost per run, and what changes at 2,000 GPUs.
  4. Public proof (posts, pull requests, a talk) shows you can communicate, which the posting stresses as much as skill.

Common mistakes

  • A repo that only works on your laptop
  • Results with no thresholds or explanation
  • Leaving cloud resources running after the demo

What interviewers probe

Walk me through your validation kit: what it checks, what it caught and what a run costs.
