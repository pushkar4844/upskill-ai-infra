Concept primer

Observability and reporting

Observability for GPU fleets means the right metrics, the right alerts and the ability to turn failure data into decisions.

Key ideas

  1. dcgm-exporter publishes DCGM fields to Prometheus: utilization, memory, power, temperature, XID errors, NVLink counters and profiling metrics (SM activity, tensor-core activity).
  2. Utilization is not efficiency. A GPU can show 100% 'utilization' while doing little useful work; profiling metrics and MFU tell the truth.
  3. Alert on XID errors, uncorrectable ECC and row-remap failures, missing GPUs, throttling and NVLink errors.
  4. Fleet metrics: failures per 1,000 GPU-days, mean time between interruptions, time to repair, cohorted by rack, firmware, driver and hardware batch.

Common mistakes

  • Dashboards with no alert rules
  • Not mapping GPU metrics to jobs or tenants
  • Reporting averages that hide one bad rack

What interviewers probe

Which three GPU metrics do you alert on, and why not utilization alone? How do you tell a bad GPU batch from a bad rack?
