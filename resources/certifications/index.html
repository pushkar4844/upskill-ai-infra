NCP-AII (NVIDIA-Certified Professional: AI Infrastructure)

NVIDIA's exam page (checked Oct 8, 2026): 120 minutes, 70-75 questions, $400, online with remote proctoring (Certiverse), valid for two years. Recommended background: two to three years of data-center operations with NVIDIA hardware; treat the labs here as your substitute and read the page before you book.

Domain weights as published in NVIDIA's exam study guide (reported by third-party guides; confirm in the official guide): Cluster Test and Verification 33%, Systems and Server Bring-up 31%, Control Plane Installation and Configuration 19%, Troubleshooting and Optimization 12%, Physical Layer Management 5%. Phases 1-3 of this roadmap line up with the first three domains.
