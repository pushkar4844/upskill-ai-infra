Environments and cost

Every lab is tagged with a code that says where it runs. Most of the path runs on a laptop or one cheap spot GPU.

How to use this roadmapWeekly plansFirst 10 daysEnvironments & cost
L

Laptop (Windows + WSL2 Ubuntu)

Rough cost: Free

Python, Docker, kind, Redfish mockups, Slurm-in-Docker, KWOK, notebooks, writing.

22 labs: A1 B1 K1 D1 G1 E3 G5 H3 H4 H5 I1 C4 D3 E4 F4 G4 H7 H8 I2 J2 J3 L2

V

Two small Linux VMs (AWS t3.large or VirtualBox)

Rough cost: ~$0.17/h for both on AWS; free on VirtualBox

Things WSL can't do: kernel modules (Soft-RoCE), perf, real NICs, PXE.

2 labs: E1 D2

G1

One GPU, spot (g4dn.xlarge T4 16 GB or g5.xlarge A10G 24 GB)

Rough cost: ~$0.20-0.50/h spot (check current price)

Drivers, DCGM, burn-in, containers, GPU Operator, vLLM, metrics.

11 labs: B2 H1 A2 A3 A4 B3 B4 C3 J1 K4 H6

G4

One multi-GPU node (g4dn.12xlarge 4xT4 or g5.12xlarge 4xA10G)

Rough cost: ~$1.2-2.5/h spot, ~$4-6/h on-demand

nccl-tests on one node, torchrun, Slurm with real GPUs, topology. No NVLink on these - PCIe only.

8 labs: C1 F1 C2 F3 G2 K2 K3 I3

G2N

Two EFA nodes in a cluster placement group (2 x g4dn.12xlarge)

Rough cost: ~$8/h on-demand for both - run 2-3 h, then destroy

EFA, multi-node NCCL, scaling, bisection. Use NVIDIA LaunchPad / a borrowed InfiniBand cluster for IB-only labs.

5 labs: E2 F2 G3 B5 L1

R

Rented A100/H100 by the hour (GPU cloud or NVIDIA LaunchPad)

Rough cost: Varies - check provider

MIG (needs A100/H100), NVLink/NVSwitch, GPUDirect RDMA, real fabric.

2 labs: E5 H2

If you keep to spot instances and destroy after each session, the full path costs a few hundred dollars. Most of that is the multi-node (G2N) and rented (R) sessions. The budget alarm from S2 and the labdown check from S10 are what keep it there.
