Why it matters

Contributions and written communication; both NVIDIA SA postings stress clear writing.

Gives evidence for these requirements in the posting:

  • Stand-out: multi-team leadership, executive communication

New to capstone and proof? Read the 5-minute primer first.

What done looks like

Deliverable. Posts, merged or open PR links, slides, updated profile.
The outcome, step by step (field manual version)
  1. Publish one short post per domain from your results (triage, XID decoder, NUMA penalty, nccl-tests scaling, acceptance runbook).
  2. Open at least two pull requests: documentation or example fixes in repos you used, for example in the AWS distributed-training samples, nccl-tests or the readiness-engine docs. Read each repo's contribution guide first.
  3. Prepare a ten-minute talk: bring-up from rack to training job, using your capstone as the story.
  4. Update your headline and About section to 'Cloud and AI infrastructure' and link the kit.

Hands-on steps

  1. One short post per domain from your own results (triage, XID decoder, NUMA penalty, NCCL scaling, acceptance runbook). Each: problem, what you did, numbers, lesson.
  2. Two pull requests: docs or example fixes in repos you used (aws-samples/awsome-distributed-training, nccl-tests, cluster-readiness-engine). Read CONTRIBUTING first; small and useful beats big.
  3. 10-minute talk: rack to training job, using the capstone as the story.
  4. Update headline/About to 'Cloud and AI infrastructure', link the capstone repo.
