Why it matters

Executive-level communication on status, risks, progress and required decisions; influence without authority.

Gives evidence for these requirements in the posting:

  • Stand-out: multi-team leadership, executive communication

New to observability and reporting? Read the 5-minute primer first.

What done looks like

Deliverable. Status template, escalation template, stakeholder map and a recording.
The outcome, step by step (field manual version)
  1. Write a weekly status for a fictional 2,048-GPU bring-up: RAG by stage, top risks, decisions needed this week.
  2. Write an escalation email with a root-cause hypothesis, evidence and ask.
  3. Make a stakeholder map for Product, Engineering, Sales, Operations and the cloud customer. For each: what they want and what you can offer.
  4. Record yourself explaining a bottleneck diagnosis in 60 seconds. Replay and cut filler.

Hands-on steps

  1. Weekly status for a fictional 2,048-GPU bring-up: RAG per stage, top 3 risks, decisions needed this week. Max one page.
  2. Escalation email: hypothesis, evidence, impact, ask, deadline.
  3. Stakeholder map: Product, Engineering, Sales, Operations, customer - what each wants, what you can offer.
  4. Record 60-second explanations of 3 diagnoses (e.g. F3 hang, C2 NUMA, E2 EFA fallback). Replay, cut filler, re-record.
  5. Map 3-4 stories from your own past projects (a migration, a DR drill, a cost-optimisation programme) to 'influence without authority' answers, each in situation-action-result form with one number.
You should see. Templates + recordings.
